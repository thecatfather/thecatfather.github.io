Raghav Bongole

Doctoral Student in Information Science and Engineering

I work on information-theoretic methods for reinforcement learning and interactive statistical decision making.

My research focuses on fundamental performance bounds, minimax regret, tail-sensitive risk measures, and information-theoretic inequalities.

I am supervised by Mikael Skoglund and Tobias J. Oechtering at KTH Royal Institute of Technology.
