Research My research lies at the intersection of information theory, statistical decision theory, and reinforcement learning. Information-theoretic limits of learning How can quantities such as mutual information and f-divergences characterize the fundamental difficulty of sequential decision problems? Minimax regret I study information-theoretic approaches to bounding the minimax regret of reinforcement-learning problems. Interactive statistical decision making I am interested in decision problems in which observations are generated through interaction with the environment, particularly lower bounds for risk-sensitive objectives. Fano-type inequalities I study extensions of Fano's inequality that can be used beyond classical probability-of-error arguments. Tail risk I am interested in risk measures such as quantiles and CVaR, and in information-theoretic methods for obtaining lower bounds for these objectives.
